Frequently asked questions

Is HelpWork free?

The app is intended to be free to download. Gemini API usage depends on your account, model and available quota.

Does it always give the correct answer?

No. AI can make mistakes, even with double-checking enabled. Review answers before relying on them.

Does it work offline?

Screenshot capture is local, but generating an answer requires an internet connection.

What happens to my screenshots?

HelpWork sends the selected screenshot to Google Gemini when you generate an answer. The app does not intentionally save screenshot files. Google processes submissions under its own terms. Avoid capturing sensitive information.

Can I use my own API key?

Yes. Keep your key private and never publish your .env file.
